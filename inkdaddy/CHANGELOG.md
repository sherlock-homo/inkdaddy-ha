## 0.2.0-dev.21

- The frame keeps a confirmed photo on screen when the same photo comes up again with identical pixels. **Refresh** still redraws it on demand.
- You can visit another inkDaddy tab and return to your unsaved dashboard. Clock widgets identify their scheduled time even under a custom title.
- **Updates** shows the latest version available in Apps, the automatic-update setting, and the firmware version Home Assistant has recorded for each display. Select the app or frame to open its native management page.

## 0.2.0-dev.20

- **Manage Photos → Framing → Intelligent Crop** frames a photo around its strongest visual detail. **Edit framing** lets you change an uploaded photo while preserving its original.
- Create photo overlays with Home Assistant readings, dates and calendar events. Set each frame’s refresh interval in **Devices**.
- **Integrations → Request Integration** saves a request locally and can deliver it to the owner's configured relay.
