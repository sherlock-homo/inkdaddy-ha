## 0.2.0-dev.27

- Six main tabs group related tools. Connect a display under **Devices**, manage Home Assistant under **Integrations**, and find updates and diagnostics under **Settings**.
- The integration request form now matches the other cards and shows feedback for the request you just made.

## 0.2.0-dev.26

- Home Overview pairs a large typeset date with illustrated current weather. The battery gauge fills with the reported percentage, and a seven-day forecast starts in Atlanta.
- Choose a forecast city and temperature unit in the widget editor. Set each dashboard’s primary and accent inks under **Dashboard colors**.

## 0.2.0-dev.25

- Restore the local **Date** tile where the original Home Status clock left an empty space. Custom layouts keep their chosen tiles.
- Keep hourly weather reports visible between Home Assistant syncs. Readings more than an hour old show the provider’s report time and expire after three hours.
- Choose **Photo details** under **Dashboards → Photo overlays** for a lower-left caption with the photo’s capture date, age and available location. Photos without those details stay clear.

## 0.2.0-dev.24

- Alternate playlist photos with a full-screen dashboard. Each gets half of the display’s configured cycle. A 60-minute cycle changes the screen every 30 minutes.
- Drag a widget edge to resize it. Choose the Home Assistant data source for supported widgets, including Location and Display battery. The dashboard no longer includes a clock.
- Add Home Assistant press buttons for Next, Previous, Play/Pause, and Refresh on the paired frame.

## 0.2.0-dev.23

- Add **Photo details** to an overlay to show where or when the current photo was taken, or how many years have passed. Existing uploads use their saved originals. Preview a photo before assigning the overlay to a display.

## 0.2.0-dev.22

- Transparent photos now use the panel’s white background in Intelligent Crop, the framing editor, and the finished image. Re-edit an existing photo to render it from its original.

## 0.2.0-dev.21

- The frame keeps a confirmed photo on screen when the same photo comes up again with identical pixels. **Refresh** still redraws it on demand.
- You can visit another inkDaddy tab and return to your unsaved dashboard. Clock widgets identify their scheduled time even under a custom title.
- **Updates** shows the latest version available in Apps, the automatic-update setting, and the firmware version Home Assistant has recorded for each display. Select the app or frame to open its native management page.

## 0.2.0-dev.20

- **Manage Photos → Framing → Intelligent Crop** frames a photo around its strongest visual detail. **Edit framing** lets you change an uploaded photo while preserving its original.
- Create photo overlays with Home Assistant readings, dates and calendar events. Set each frame’s refresh interval in **Devices**.
- **Integrations → Request Integration** saves a request locally and can deliver it to the owner's configured relay.
